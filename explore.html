After learning the five structural steps, you can explore three complete solving sessions that demonstrate the Visual Structural Method from scramble to solution.

Each session begins with a unique scramble generated using Twizzle tools and follows the same Foundation → Walls → Windows progression taught in the book. These examples show how the cube develops through structure recognition, visual pattern moves, and guided progression.

These examples illustrate how the method performs across different scrambles while maintaining a consistent structural approach.

Move counts and speed are not part of the teaching method. The sessions simply provide an opportunity to observe the method in practice and experience its calm, predictable, and visually guided nature.
